EV Charging Slot Availability System

An intelligent web application designed to eliminate EV driver range anxiety, eliminate station queues, and streamline charger scheduling.

The Problem

EV users frequently face range anxiety and unpredictable charging availability. Upon arriving at public charging stations, drivers often discover occupied connectors, malfunctioning chargers, or lengthy waiting lines. This leads to substantial time waste, disrupted travel schedules, and suboptimal infrastructure utilization.

The Solution

Our platform empowers EV drivers to locate nearby charging hubs via interactive OpenStreetMap, inspect live socket telemetry (Available, Occupied, Maintenance), pre-book time slots to guarantee charger availability, and receive AI-driven recommendations based on distance, queue wait times, and tariffs.

Backend Server
